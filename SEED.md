# SEED — the input

Steward-owned. This file is the only required input to the whole system.
Fill it in rough notes — the loops do the rest. Everything here is a
starting hypothesis, not a commitment; research exists to kill or confirm.

## Problem
<what hurts, for whom — one paragraph, plain words>

## Audience
<who has the problem; who pays; how you reach them>

## Constraints
<budget, stack, timeline, regulatory — the non-negotiables>

## Non-goals
<what this is explicitly NOT, so research doesn't wander>

## Initial questions
The researcher works this list and appends new ones to records/questions/.
- q-001: <who already solves this, and what do their users complain about?>
- q-002: <what do people currently pay / substitute with?>
- q-003: <what signal would prove nobody wants this?>

## Success signal
<the observable that tells you the market said yes — replies, signups,
payments, usage. Pick something countable; "people like it" is not a
signal.>
